First Play release.

• Audits every Flutter app on the device and grades it A–F from the APK itself.
• Reports SDKs, AI providers, endpoints, routes, secrets, signing, network security and permissions.
• Picks up single APKs too: pick or share one and get the same report.
• Secrets stay masked, and revealing one blocks screenshots.
• Fully offline: no network permission, no analytics, no account.
• Privacy policy and security model are readable in Settings.
